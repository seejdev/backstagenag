Experience the soulful original music of Juliet Hawkins full band, as she shares her own personal stories of hope, healing, and the human experience through song, with authentic live performance. A sound that blends jazz, RnB and a touch of the old school gritty country western, she’ll be sharing songs from her full catalogue, including tunes off her live record, Stay True, engineered by Grammy winner Bob Tucker (the Temptations, Tupac), as well as recent singles like the sultry reggae jam “Lucy”, and “Mad Men”, recorded right next door at Rusty Door Recording. With Wes Stannard on guitar, Spanky Roman on bass, Lance Comer on drums and Jake McLaughlin on keys. Opening the show is Nick Buchard. $10 in advance, $12 at the door.
