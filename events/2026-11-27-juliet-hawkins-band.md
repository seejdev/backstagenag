A full-band night of original music written by Juliet Hawkins. She sings and plays guitar, with Wes Stannard on guitar, Spanky Roman on bass, and Lance Comer on drums.

Hawkins is a Vermont singer-songwriter who writes her own songs and reworks classics. Her live album Stay True was recorded with a band in California. Recent singles include Mad Men, recorded at Rusty Door in Londonderry, and Lucy.
