The Hayley Jane Band blends the raw power of rock, the soul of folk, and the dramatic intensity of a great film score into a genre-defying performance that’s as heartfelt as it is electrifying. Frontwoman Hayley Jane dances the spectrum between siren and storyteller, carving out a space where vulnerability meets showmanship and music becomes a visceral experience.

Known for high-energy live shows that pull you into their world, the band features Hayley Jane on vocals alongside guitarist Jackson Bower, drummer Sam Lyons, bassist Tom Gladstone, and pianist Parker McQueeney. Their original music combines poetic lyrics, driving grooves, and explosive dynamics — exploring personal transformation, collective rebellion, and everything in between. It’s music for misfits and misunderstoods, lovers and fighters.

Critics have praised Hayley’s infectious energy, powerful vocal range, and theatrical stage presence rooted in musical theater. As Relix Magazine put it: “Hayley Jane captivates her audiences with the kind of theatrics usually reserved for Mick Jagger or Janis Joplin.”

Based out of Burlington, Vermont, the Hayley Jane Band leaves a lasting mark whether on a festival stage or in an intimate room. Audiences don’t just watch — they feel it.

Doors at 6:00 PM, show at 8:00 PM.
