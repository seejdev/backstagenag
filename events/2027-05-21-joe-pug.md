A singer-songwriter known for sharp lyrics and a plaintive harmonica. Joe Pug has released a string of records since 2008 and has shared stages with Steve Earle, Levon Helm, Sturgill Simpson, and Justin Townes Earle. Paste Magazine once wrote that unless your surname is Dylan, Waits, Ritter, or Prine, you could face-palm yourself trying to write songs half as inspired.

He has played Lollapalooza, Bonnaroo, and the Newport Folk Festival, and he hosts the podcast The Working Songwriter.
