Backstage @ NAG is an evolution of the live-music room at [The New American Grill](https://www.thenewamericangrill.com/) in Londonderry, Vermont — a full stage and a proper sound system, lights, (smoke machine but don't tell anyone) off the side of a Route 100 restaurant that has been a locals' favorite for more than twenty years.

Big Acts, Small Room.

Grab a table in the dining room or a seat at the bar, then walk into the room when the band starts.
