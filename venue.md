Backstage @ NAG is an evolution of the live-music room at [The New American Grill](https://www.thenewamericangrill.com/) in Londonderry, Vermont — a full stage and pro level PA, lights, (smoke machine but don't tell anyone) in the back of a Route 100 restaurant that has been a locals' favorite for more than twenty years.

Come for dinner, stay for the show. Grab a table in the dining room or a seat at the bar, then head back when the band starts.
